# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in AgenticVisor to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps build a welcoming community:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Accepting feedback on code and ideas, and giving it kindly.
- Assuming good intent, and asking when something is unclear.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who use the project.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks, in any form.
- Publishing private information about others without their consent, including
  conversations, credentials or personal data posted as examples.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the
repository's **Security** tab (private vulnerability reporting), or by contacting
the repository owner. Every report will be reviewed. Maintainers may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, issues or other contributions that do not
follow this code, and may ban contributors who repeatedly break it.
